An AI-powered inbox assistant that reads your team's emails, finds the action items hiding inside them, and makes sure nothing falls through the cracks.

The Problem

In large software programs, critical action items are buried in hundreds of daily emails — status updates, meeting notes, vendor requests, change orders, and more. Important tasks get missed, deadlines slip, and teams waste hours manually tracking who needs to do what.

The bigger the program, the worse it gets. A single missed action item in an email can cascade into schedule delays, compliance issues, or costly rework.

How INtelliBOX Solves It

INtelliBOX uses artificial intelligence to automatically read every email your team receives, understand what's being asked, and extract the specific action items — who needs to do what, by when, and how urgent it is.

Instead of relying on people to manually read, interpret, and track every email, the AI does it instantly and consistently, 24/7.

Why AI Changes Everything

Consider a large software program with 500+ emails per week across multiple teams, vendors, and stakeholders. A human would need to carefully read each email, decide which ones contain action items, categorize them by urgency, figure out who's responsible, and enter them into a tracking system. That's easily 10–20 hours of work per week — and things still get missed.

INtelliBOX does the same work in minutes, with consistent accuracy, and never takes a day off. It doesn't replace your team's judgment — it gives them a complete, organized picture so they can focus on doing the work instead of finding it.
